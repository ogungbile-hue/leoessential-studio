## 2. Core Pillars & Business Model

### A. Bespoke Lash Architecture
Leoessential refuses mass-market, assembly-line lash application. Every procedure follows strict biometric mapping:
- **1:1 Medical Isolation:** Absolute zero-follicle clumping to guarantee indefinite lash preservation.
- **Follicular Weight Matching:** Extension diameters (.05mm to .12mm) engineered to prevent natural lash traction alopecia.
- **Custom Curvature:** Tailored to orbital depth, brow arch, and eyelid fold (Classic Minimalist, Textured Hybrid, Velvet Volume, Editorial Wispy).

### B. Semi-Permanent Brow Artistry
High-precision cosmetic tattooing and non-invasive brow grooming:
- **Ombré Powder Microshading:** Single-needle rotary device depositing organic pigments in an airbrushed gradient (lasts 18–24 months).
- **Brow Sculpt & Keratin Lamination:** Directional restructuring and custom undertone tinting.

### C. 1:1 Professional Mentorship Academy
Private, intensive masterclasses capped at 2 students per month:
- **Foundational Masterclasses:** 2-Day and 3-Day immersions covering cyanoacrylate polymer chemistry, hygrometry, ergonomics, and live-model supervision.
- **Speed & Retention Diagnostic Clinics:** Advanced troubleshooting for practicing technicians.
- **Business Blueprint:** Client waiver architecture, Square deposit policies, and luxury pricing psychology.

### D. In-Studio Curated Retail
Exclusive, ophthalmologist-tested post-procedure aftercare formulated for lash wearers:
- Foaming botanical lash cleansers, 22-momme Mulberry silk contoured eye masks, 120k micro-bristle wash brushes, and botanical barrier sealants.
- **Operational Model:** Strictly in-studio pickup (reserve via WhatsApp/Square prior to appointment). No e-commerce shipping gateway required.

- **Strict Foreign Work Policy:** Leoessential does not execute refills over outside work; clients with foreign sets must book a Gentle Removal + Fresh Full Set.
- **Dual Currency Requirement:** Dual transparency with primary NGN (`₦`) and secondary USD (`$`) for diaspora and international masterclass candidates.
- **Punctuality Grace Period:** Strict 10-to-15 minute threshold before appointment curtailment or forfeiture.
