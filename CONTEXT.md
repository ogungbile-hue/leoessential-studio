## 2. Core Pillars & Business Model

### A. Bespoke Lash Architecture (Core Discipline)
Leoessential refuses mass-market, assembly-line lash application. Every set follows strict biometric mapping:
- **1:1 Medical Isolation:** Absolute zero-follicle clumping to guarantee indefinite natural lash preservation.
- **Follicular Weight Matching:** Extension diameters (.05mm to .12mm) engineered to prevent natural lash traction alopecia.
- **Custom Curvature & Eye Mapping:** Tailored to orbital depth, brow arch, and eyelid fold (Classic Minimalist, Textured Hybrid, Velvet Volume, Editorial Wispy Kim-K Spikes).
- **Lash Maintenance & Refill Rituals:** Dedicated bi-weekly appointments to fortify retention, clarify roots with peptide foam baths, and replace outgrown fibers.
- **Gentle Keratin Removal:** Safe, non-invasive adhesive breakdown without mechanical stress.

### B. 1:1 Professional Lash Mentorship Academy
Private, intensive masterclasses capped at 2 students per month:
- **Foundational Masterclasses:** 2-Day immersions covering cyanoacrylate polymer chemistry, hygrometry, ergonomics, and live-model supervision.
- **Speed & Retention Diagnostic Clinics:** Advanced troubleshooting for practicing lash technicians.
- **Business Blueprint:** Client waiver architecture, Square deposit policies, and luxury pricing psychology.
- **Accreditation:** Leoessential Certificate of Professional Lash Artistry.

### C. In-Studio Curated Lash Care Retail
Exclusive, ophthalmologist-tested post-procedure aftercare formulated for lash wearers:
- Foaming botanical lash cleansers, 22-momme Mulberry silk contoured eye masks, 120k micro-bristle wash brushes, and peptide barrier sealants.
- **Operational Model:** Strictly in-studio pickup (reserve via WhatsApp/Square prior to appointment). No e-commerce shipping gateway required.
